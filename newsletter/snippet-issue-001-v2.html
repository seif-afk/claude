Why 90% of Merch Companies Fail at Lead Gen

The merch industry has a lead generation problem. Here's what the top 10% do differently — and how you can steal their playbook.

Lead Generation Network

This Week's Insight

The Funnel Nobody Talks About

Most merch companies spend 80% of their marketing budget on cold outreach — blasting emails to bought lists, running generic ads, and praying someone bites. The conversion rate? Usually under 0.5%.

Meanwhile, the top performers have flipped the script. They build what I call the “Gravity Funnel” — a system where leads come to them.

Here’s how it works:

Step 1: Create a “lead magnet” that solves a real problem. Not a generic PDF — something like a custom merch ROI calculator or a packaging cost estimator.

Step 2: Gate it behind an email capture on your website. Use a dedicated landing page, not your homepage.

Step 3: Nurture with value. Send 3–5 emails over two weeks that teach something — before you ever pitch.

The companies doing this see 5–12x higher conversion rates than the cold outreach crowd. And they spend less money doing it.

Key Takeaways
1

Stop buying lists

Cold lists are dead. Build your own audience with tools that give before they ask. A lead magnet with real utility outperforms 10,000 cold emails.
2

Nurture before you pitch

3–5 value-driven emails before your first CTA. Teach them something they can use today. The trust you build compounds.
3

Measure gravity, not volume

Stop tracking how many emails you sent. Track how many leads came to you organically. That’s the metric that predicts revenue.
